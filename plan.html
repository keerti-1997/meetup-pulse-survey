Monthly Survey & RSVP Check-In — Plan

A recurring tool survey for AI Tinkerers Women in AI, filled during the networking window, plus a QR-based check-in system built on an existing RSVP list. Everything below is a proposal — react to any of it and I'll adjust before we build.

01Why this stack

You need identity-linked responses, trend tracking across months, and QR check-in — without taking on a paid platform or ongoing server maintenance.

OptionVerdictWhy
Lovable appSkipSolves this, but adds hosting cost and a platform dependency for something that's really just a form + a spreadsheet.
GitHub Pages + Google FormsSkipFree and fast, but Forms can't easily pre-associate a response with a specific attendee token — you asked for identity tied to RSVP.
GitHub Pages + Google Sheets/Apps ScriptRecommendedStatic site is free on GitHub Pages; Sheets + Apps Script acts as a free backend you can already read/edit by hand. Supports personalized tokens, check-in state, and multi-month trend rows in one place.

02How it flows

Two QR codes per attendee, generated once from your RSVP export, printed on their badge.

  1. Import the RSVP listDrop your platform's export (name + email, minimum) into a small import page. It assigns each attendee a random token.
  2. Generate two QR codes per personA check-in QR (organizer-facing) and a survey QR (attendee-facing), printed on a badge or table card.
  3. Door: organizer scans check-in QRPhone camera + browser page, no app install. Marks that attendee present in the Attendees sheet, in real time.
  4. Networking: attendee scans their own survey QROpens their personal, pre-linked survey. Answers are written to the Responses sheet, tagged with their token and the event date.
  5. After the eventBoth sheets accumulate one tagged row set per month, so a simple chart tab shows trends — tool adoption, agent counts — over time.

05Open questions before I build

Confirm or correct these assumptions

  • RSVP export format — I'm assuming your custom platform can export a CSV with at minimum name + email. If it's missing email, the survey QR still works (token-based), but you'd lose a fallback way to re-send a badge.
  • Badge delivery — assuming both QR codes are printed on a badge or table card handed out at check-in, not emailed ahead of time. Say if you'd rather email the survey QR in advance so people can fill it before they even arrive.
  • Cadence — assuming "monthly" means one event per month, one importer run each time, all feeding the same two sheets with an event-date column for trend charts.
  • Who maintains it — this needs a one-time setup (~2–3 hrs: Sheet + Apps Script + GitHub Pages), then near-zero upkeep per month beyond running the CSV import. Confirm that's you, or another organizer.
  • Public reporting — you said not sure yet. Since raw data lives in a normal Sheet, flipping from private to a public recap later is just a pivot table, no rebuild needed.

06If you say go — build phases

1 — Backend

Google Sheet with Attendees + Responses tabs, Apps Script Web App exposing check-in and submit-response endpoints.

2 — Site

GitHub Pages: survey form page, camera-based scanner page, both reading/writing through the Apps Script API.

3 — QR tooling

Client-side importer: upload your RSVP CSV, get back a printable sheet of badges with two QR codes each.

4 — Dry run

Test the full loop at the next meetup — check-in scan, survey scan, data lands correctly — before trusting it live.

5 — Trends view

A simple chart tab or page reading the Responses sheet, once 2–3 months of data exist to compare.
